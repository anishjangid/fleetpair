# fleetpair — 3-minute demo video script

**Total runtime:** ~3:00  
**Login:** `ananya@indiranagar.hub` / `fleetpair`  
**Tone:** calm floor manager, not pitch-deck voice  
**Setup before record:** board open, wrong long-pack match ready (Asha on long pack if recommended is Ravi), mic permission allowed if you show voice

---

## Shot list (what’s on screen)

| Time | Screen |
|------|--------|
| 0:00–0:20 | Login → Home |

## Full script (speak this)

### [0:00–0:20] Hook + login

**Do:** Open login. Type credentials. Land on Morning board.

**Say:**  
“Every morning at an EV rental hub, riders show up for scooters and packs. Days aren’t fixed routes — they’re usage patterns. Wrong match means a stranded rider, lost rent, and a pack that wears out faster. This is fleetpair — the morning board for that decision.”

---

### [0:20–0:50] Home = do these first

**Do:** Point at the three numbers (Covered / Service 30d / To do). Tap one checklist action — Book bay or Change rider. Show toast / sheet briefly. Skip one item if useful.

**Say:**  
“Home isn’t another analytics wall. It’s what to do first. Covered bookings, expected service spend for the next thirty days, and a short job list — book the bay, change a rider, fix a match. Managers need actions, not charts.”

---

### [0:50–1:25] Matches — the heart of the product

**Do:** Open Matches. Show Asha vs Ravi on the long pack. Switch assignment. Pause so covered / rent numbers update. Hit recommended / fix long pack if available.

**Say:**  
“Here’s the hard call. Asha runs dense swaps and is hard on the bike. Ravi has one long stretch between dependable swaps. Only one pack has the usable range for that gap. We match on behaviour — day kilometres and swap gap versus pack usable range — not on who looks free. Flip the assignment and you see bookings and rent at stake. Same logic scales to the full hub.”

---

### [1:25–1:50] Map

**Do:** Open Map. Filter “Service soon” then “Value risk”. Tap a marker / list row. Open bike if sheet opens.

**Say:**  
“Map puts each bike in the rider’s usual zone from past shifts — Koramangala, Whitefield, and so on. That’s context for the floor. Matching still isn’t ‘nearest pin’ — it’s range versus behaviour. Filters pull service-soon and value-risk units fast.”

---

### [1:50–2:20] Service & cost

**Do:** Open Service. Point at Spent vs next 30 days. Scroll one behaviour issue. Tap Book bay or Mark fixed.

**Say:**  
“Service connects past rider and bike behaviour to open issues and cost. Here’s what we’ve spent this month versus what’s expected next thirty days if we don’t act. Issues aren’t generic tickets — they come from how the unit was used.”

---

### [2:20–2:40] Residual / bikes (pick one)

**Option A — Bikes:** Open Bikes, open a weak unit, show condition + residual at risk.  
**Option B — Rent:** Open Rent, point at value at risk.

**Say:**  
“Wrong pairing doesn’t only hurt today’s rent. It burns pack health and pulls residual value down. Ops and the lease book finally share one language.”

*(Optional 8 sec: tap the mic → “fix long pack” or “open map”.)*

---

### [2:40–3:00] Close

**Do:** Return to Home. Hold on Morning board. End.

**Say:**  
“fleetpair: match riders to scooters and packs on real usage behaviour, then tell the hub what to do next about service and cost — so the morning board protects today’s rent and the lease book. Thanks for watching.”

---

## Condensed cue card (print this)

1. **Hook** — wrong match = stranded + rent + residual  
2. **Home** — checklist, not KPIs  
3. **Matches** — Asha/Ravi long pack; behaviour not routes  
4. **Map** — usual zones; matching still behaviour  
5. **Service** — spent vs next 30d; issues from use  
6. **Residual** — ops meets lease book  
7. **Close** — protect today’s rent and the lease book  

---

## Recording tips

- Speak slightly slower than normal; leave 1 beat after each screen change  
- Don’t explain every button — one action per section is enough  
- If you overrun: cut Map filters to one, skip voice, skip Rent  
- If you underrun: show voice command “fix long pack” after Matches  
- Caption burn-in ideas: `behaviour > routes` · `morning checklist` · `protect residual`
