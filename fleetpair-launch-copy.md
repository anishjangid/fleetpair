**full project description**

fleetpair is a hub handover tool for teams that rent electric two-wheelers to delivery riders. days aren’t fixed routes — the product reads usage patterns from rider and vehicle behaviour (typical day km, observed swap gaps, wear, pack health, scooter readiness) and matches each rider to a scooter and battery that can cover the shift. switch assignments to see bookings and rent at stake. a 20-second challenge mode (asha / ravi) is the shareable demo of the same decision. the riders, scores and rental amounts are simulated.

## 30-second live demo pitch

“you’re running an ev rental hub. asha has 100 km of deliveries today, ravi has 55. who gets the 65 km scooter?”

take a vote. give it to asha first. “ravi is 15 km short. here’s the detail that changes the decision. asha can swap every 25 km, while ravi needs to finish his 55 km route on one pack.”

tap **switch scooters**. “now both bookings fit. at this demo tariff, the same two scooters could support ₹500 in rent today instead of ₹250.” open **why this match?** to show the technician handover recommendation for asha.
